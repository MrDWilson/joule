# Code of conduct

Joule follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not welcome in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainer privately through the email address on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/MrDWilson), or open a private [security advisory](https://github.com/MrDWilson/joule/security/advisories/new) if no address is listed. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly do so. The enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant apply.
